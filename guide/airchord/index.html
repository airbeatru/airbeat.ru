AirChord: chords and sounds

Large pads, key, chord variants, five play modes, ChordSequencer, effects and presets.

Play harmony with seven pads

Open Jam → AirChord. In KEY choose key, scale, octave and the chord's bass-note options; choose a timbre in SOUND. The separate bassline instrument is AirBass.

Pads 1–7 are degrees of the current key, not fixed chord names. In C major, pads 6, 4, 1, 5 give Am–F–C–G. Press and hold a pad; releasing it ends an ordinary note.

In Instrument settings, choose Chord variant: root voicing, seventh, sus4 and others. Pad names reflect the selected variant. You can hold several pads at once. Playing view helps on a small screen; scroll using the margins around the pads.

Choose a play mode

In Instrument settings → Presets & Motion, choose arpeggio direction, rate 1/8 or 1/16, and octave range. Auto retains the original timing grid across contexts. Gate controls step/arpeggio hold time, Attack the onset and Release the ending. Envelope Auto retains the timbre's original character.

ModeBehaviour
ChordChord notes start together.
StrumNotes start in sequence, like a strum.
ArpeggioA repeated note pattern while a pad is held.
DroneThe chord latches after release; Stop all clears it.
LeadA pad plays a melodic note.

ChordSequencer and effects

  1. Expand and enable ChordSequencer. Choose a chord in a column; an empty column is a rest. Sixteen steps form one bar.
  2. Use Tie mode to sustain across steps. Adjust Gate and Swing, then press Play on the shared Jam transport.
  3. Open Instrument settings, enable Reverb, Delay or Chorus and adjust the amount. Reverb adds reflections, Delay repeats and Chorus movement and width. AirChord volume sets its balance; Master affects the whole mix.

Sounds, progressions and user presets differ

A factory sound preset loads timbre, Mode, envelope and effects without replacing key, tempo, volume or pattern. A progression loads only a 16-step pattern in the current key.

A user preset stores key, scale, sound, effects, Motion and steps with Ties. It does not replace tempo, volume, sequencer on/off. Save it under your own name; presets belong to project files. Loading and deleting support Undo.

To change harmony in only one composition block, use its Arrangement inspector.
