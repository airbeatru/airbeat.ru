Jam scenes: verse and chorus

Four variations of three instruments, shared tempo, bar transitions and scene history.

Store a verse and a chorus

  1. Open More → Jam scenes. Stop Jam, prepare patterns and sounds for the three instruments, then press Save here in the first slot.
  2. Enter a name such as Verse and press Enter or leave the field. Names allow up to 40 characters and do not change with interface language.
  3. Change the music and store another version in another slot, such as Chorus. There are four slots. Save here replaces that slot with the current playing; scene Undo reverses the action.

What a scene retains

A scene stores patterns and sounds for AirBeat, AirBass and AirChord. It is an independent snapshot: later manual edits do not rewrite it automatically. A hint marks differences between current playing and the active scene.

BPM, Master and master mix processing remain shared. Your kit/preset libraries and Arrangement blocks stay independent. Scenes do not record knob movements or automatically create an arrangement.

Selecting a stopped scene only prepares instruments. Press Play to start Jam.

Switch while playing

  1. Start Jam and select a populated scene. Next bar identifies the pending version; it applies on a bar boundary.
  2. Cancel transition keeps the current playing. Selecting the queued scene again does not postpone it; choosing an unchanged current scene cancels the pending choice.
  3. You can close the panel; the pending name stays in its heading. Pause, Stop, workspace or BPM changes cancel the queue. A severely delayed transition is skipped with a message; select the scene again.

Clear, history and project files

Names, saving, clearing and Undo/Redo require stopped Jam. Save your current playing in a separate slot before replacing a scene.

Clear frees the slot while retaining the current music; scene Undo restores the slot. Undoing a scene selection cannot overwrite newer manual edits: save the current playing and explicitly select the version you want.

Project Save downloads all four scenes with the music. Sketch links do not include scenes. Older projects open with empty slots while retaining their music.
