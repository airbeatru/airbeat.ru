Mix & Dynamics+

AirBeat's global mixer applies to Jam, every Arrangement block and WAV. Multiple Solo tracks can play together; Mute takes priority. Preview bypasses Mute/Solo, but follows Level and Pan.

Steps toggles hits. Accent cycles normal → accented → soft → rest. Shift-click a step does the same. Fine-tune the selected step's Velocity below.

Kick / 01

Probability decides whether the whole step plays. Roll divides its swung duration into 1–4 equal hits. 0% is silent; 100% always plays. The probability stream is repeatable from the same start and position; new loop passes add variations. Pause resumes at the next step. Banks, Capture AirBeat, Undo and project files retain these settings.

Click a grid step or navigate with arrows to select it. Rests stay silent. Velocity 100 preserves the original level; 127 adds an accent.

Copies hits, Velocity, Probability and Roll, leaving sounds and mixer settings untouched. Shift wraps around. Paste, Shift and Clear can be undone.

Banks & Kits+

Pattern banks

Copy replaces the target bank's pattern, Velocity, Probability, Roll and Swing; Undo restores it. Tempo and mixer are shared, sounds are separate. In Jam, switches wait for a new bar. When stopped or in AirStudio, selection is immediate.

Chain

A→B→C→D

One bank per bar, up to 16 entries. Play after Stop starts at the first entry; Pause retains position. Manual bank selection disables Chain. AirStudio uses its own Arrangement blocks, not this chain.

Drum kits 0/16

A kit stores six sounds, Level and Pan. Pattern, tempo, Mute and Solo stay unchanged. Up to 16 kits live inside this project, included in drafts and Save files, not a cloud library. Load and Delete can be undone.

AirStudio owns playback and tempo. Patterns here are your scratchpad; use “Capture AirBeat” in the block editor to place one in the arrangement.

How to sequence

01 / STEPSTap a step to add a hit. Tap again to remove it. Arrow keys move between steps.

02 / GROOVEChoose from 12 patterns. Each loads its tempo and swing. Six tracks offer four sounds each; choose a variant or tap its name to preview it.

03 / UNDOUndo / Redo remembers 32 AirBeat edits, including Clear, presets, sound choices and local settings. Ctrl / Cmd + Z undoes; add Shift to redo. Studio tempo is global and is not changed by pattern Undo. Use Save for a project backup. Each slider drag or held arrow key is one Undo step. Separate key presses remain separate edits.

04 / PLAY TOGETHERStart AirBeat, then play AirChord. Both stay live when you scroll. Stop all or Esc silences both.

Presets & Motion+
AirChord presets and motion

Sound presets

Sound, Mode, envelope and effects, without changing key, tempo, volume or pattern. Loading does not start audio; Undo restores the previous settings.

Progressions

16-step patterns follow your current key and scale. Numbers are scale degrees. Ties sustain chords; empty steps are rests. Only the Jam pattern changes, not Arrangement blocks. Enable Sequencer, then press Play.

Motion

Direction, Rate and Octaves apply in Arpeggio mode: pads, Jam and Arrangement/WAV. Gate controls Jam step length and arpeggio notes. Auto preserves the original grid: eighths on pads and in Arrangement, sixteenths in legacy Jam. Explicit 1/8 and 1/16 are consistent across modes.

Auto retains each sound's original envelope. Attack controls the fade-in; Release controls the tail after note-off. Very short notes limit Attack; Stop always silences sound quickly.

User presets 0/16

A preset stores key, scale, sound, effects, Motion and steps with Ties. Tempo, volume, Sequencer on/off and the recorded Looper phrase stay unchanged. Presets are included in drafts and Save files; Load and Delete support Undo.

Recording options

Count-in gives four clicks before recording starts; the clicks are not recorded. In free mode without count-in, Record starts with your first chord. Quantization affects the next take or Overdub layer; Quantize phrase changes the stopped phrase. Undo restores the previous phrase. These options last for this session only.

With sync enabled, start AirBeat at the same BPM first. Play, Record and Overdub join a future bar; count-in adds one bar before recording. AirBeat Pause or Stop cancels an unfinished Overdub and stops Looper without automatic resume. A tempo change restarts playback at a new bar. Synced phrases must contain whole bars.

Stop the phrase before Overdub. It plays once while you add notes. Done saves the new layer and stops; Cancel keeps the original. An empty take changes nothing. Quantization changes only the new layer. Undo removes the last layer. Repeated Overdub is limited by phrase density; this is not continuous recording.

MIDI input

Web MIDI is not available here. Try a supporting browser over HTTPS; the on-screen pads still work.

White keys C D E F G A B trigger pads 1–7 in the current AirChord key; all octaves repeat those pads. Black keys, MIDI clock and pitch bend are ignored. Positive Velocity uses the pad level; zero Velocity releases the note. CC64 sustains notes; CC120/123 releases that channel. Drone keeps its usual latch; Release MIDI notes clears it.

Only the selected input is used. Browser permission covers Web MIDI, but this app does not open outputs or send MIDI data. SysEx and software synths are disabled. Device names and connections are not saved in projects. Leaving the tab releases MIDI notes; return to it before playing again. Disconnect stops listening but does not revoke browser permission.

How to play

01 / PLAYHold any of the seven chord pads. On a computer, use A S D F G H J.

02 / SHAPEHold a chord and move the joystick. Use arrow keys on a computer, or a second finger on your phone.

03 / LOOPPress Record, play a progression and turn it into a loop. Add live chords over your phrase.

Block effectsFollow AirChord effects+

Local effects replace AirChord effects for this block, including recorded Looper notes. Reverb, Delay and Chorus have separate wet sends; dry sound and AirChord volume stay shared. Delay follows studio BPM; local Chorus has a fixed modulation depth and adjustable wet mix. Previous tails continue naturally. Turn local effects off to follow AirChord again.

Tonality & levelFollow AirChord key+

Local tonality stores this block's key, scale and voicing independently of AirChord. Sound, step timing and effects are configured in separate panels; transport stays shared.

Block level scales this block before its effects and AirChord volume. 100% keeps its original level; 0% is silent. It does not change live pads or other blocks.

Block soundFollow AirChord+

Follow AirChord uses its live sound settings. Capture sound or a preset creates a separate voice for this block, without changing its progression or starting playback. Presets copy sound, Mode, octave, Bass, arpeggio and envelope only. Tonality, level and effects are configured separately.

How AirStudio works

One clock drives both tracks. Tempo changes join on the next unscheduled beat; arrangement and loop edits join on the next bar. Click a bar number to choose the next start position, then Stop / Play to restart there.

Drag blocks with a mouse, drag their right edge to resize, or use Start beat and Length on any device. Drum blocks snap to whole bars. Blocks on the same track cannot overlap. Duplicate / repeat places a separate copy in the next free space.

Step progressions follow AirChord unless local tonality or sound is set. Recorded Looper blocks keep their MIDI pitches and sounds. Each chord block can have its own level and effects; AirChord volume stays shared.

Switch to Jam to use the step sequencers and your retained loop. Backgrounding the tab or audio interruption stops playback; it never restarts automatically. Drafts stay on this device. Save downloads a backup; Open restores a project without starting playback.
